\# User Journey – Manufacturing SOP AI Copilot



\## 1. Who will use this application?



I designed this application for manufacturing employees such as machine operators, quality inspectors, maintenance technicians, and safety personnel who need to access manufacturing SOP documents.



\## 2. What problem do they face?



Employees may need to search through multiple PDF documents to find machine specifications, maintenance procedures, quality inspection details, and safety instructions. Manually searching these documents takes time and makes it difficult to find the required information quickly.



\## 3. How do they use the chatbot?



Users can open my Manufacturing SOP AI Copilot application and enter their questions in simple natural language. For example, they can ask about TX-200 coolant concentration, hydraulic pressure, or Lockout/Tagout procedures.



\## 4. How does the chatbot provide answers?



My application uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to retrieve relevant information from manufacturing PDF documents. It generates answers based on the retrieved content and provides the source document name and page number for verification.



\## 5. What happens when information is unavailable?



If the required information is not available in the uploaded documents, the chatbot displays a message saying that it could not find sufficient information to answer the question. This helps avoid generating incorrect or unsupported answers.



\## 6. How does this application help employees?



Through this project, I aim to reduce the time spent searching through manufacturing documents and make important information easier to access. Employees can quickly find machine parameters, inspection requirements, and safety procedures through a simple chatbot interface. It also helps them verify answers using the original document references.



The application is designed to support information retrieval and does not replace official SOP documents or authorized safety and maintenance decisions.
